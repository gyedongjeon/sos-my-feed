## Request

The last of three features for daily use (read state, refreshing on a schedule, OPML import and export): bring the subscriptions from another feed reader, and take them to one or keep them as a backup, with an OPML file.

## Changes

### Backend (`backend/app/opml.py`)
- `GET /api/opml` gives the subscriptions as an OPML 2.0 file, `sos-my-feed.opml`, one `outline` per feed (`text`, `title`, `type="rss"`, `xmlUrl`), in the order they were subscribed to.
- `POST /api/opml` takes the text of an OPML file as the request body and lists the feeds it holds (`title`, `url`), without subscribing to them. Feeds in folders are listed too, each address once, and only `http` and `https` addresses. The title is the outline's `text`, else its `title`, else the address's host. A file that is not OPML is refused (422), and one over 5 MB too (413).
- The body is the file's text rather than an uploaded file, so no new dependency (`python-multipart`) is needed.

### Frontend
- Settings → Subscriptions has **Import OPML** (a file chooser) and **Export OPML** (shown once there is a subscription).
- Importing lists the file's feeds, leaves out those already subscribed to, and subscribes to the rest the way the popular feeds are subscribed to: together, with the feeds that cannot be read named. Settings now shows that message under the buttons.
- Six new texts, in all 19 languages.
